Algorithm
1. Start.
2. Declare the variables purchase, discount, netAmount, delivery, total, customer, and distance.
3. Read the purchase amount.
4. Read the customer type (1 = Student, 2 = Regular Customer).
5. Read the delivery distance in kilometres.
6. Using conditional statements, determine the discount based on customer type and purchase amount.
7. Calculate the discount amount and the amount after discount.
8. Using conditional statements, determine the delivery charge based on distance.
9. Calculate the final payable amount by adding the delivery charge to the amount after discount.
10. Display the purchase amount, discount amount, amount after discount, delivery charge, and final payable amount.
11. Stop.
